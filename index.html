SpaceMaker

Don’t buy more storage. Create it.

SpaceMaker is an iOS-first app being designed to help people reclaim phone storage without sending their personal media to our servers. A free on-device scan estimates how much phone storage could be reclaimed, and supported photos and videos can be optimized locally after a planned one-time lifetime unlock.
