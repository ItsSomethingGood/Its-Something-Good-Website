Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Its Something Good LLC respects your privacy. This policy covers our website, itssomethinggood.net, and our iPhone app, Phone Storage Reclaimer. Privacy information for other apps will be provided separately.

Phone Storage Reclaimer

Photos and videos stay under your control

Phone Storage Reclaimer scans and processes photos and videos on your iPhone. The app does not upload your photo or video files to Its Something Good LLC or to a server for scanning or optimization.

The app accesses your Photos library only with your permission. If you grant Limited Photos access, it can scan only the items you allow it to access, and its storage estimates reflect that accessible selection rather than your entire library. You can change this permission in your iPhone’s Settings.

Scanning does not replace or delete your originals. When you choose to optimize an item, the app prepares a space-saving copy for review. Replacing an original requires your approval and the applicable Photos permission.

Information stored on your device

To display scan results and manage optimization, the app stores information locally, including storage estimates, media types and sizes, creation dates, local Photos identifiers, and the status of optimization work.

The app also creates local previews and prepared media copies and stores records used to associate those copies with their originals. These records help the app resume work and check that the correct media is being used.

This information is not sent to us as part of scanning or optimization.

Your iPhone’s backups and any iCloud Photos syncing are separate Apple services controlled by your Apple account and device settings. Changes you approve in Photos may sync to other devices if you use iCloud Photos. This policy does not change Apple’s handling of those services.

Purchases and restoring access

You do not need a Phone Storage Reclaimer account. The app is free to download and scan. Lifetime Access is a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks optimization; it is not a subscription.

Apple processes purchases. The app uses Apple’s StoreKit service to display the current localized price and verify or restore your purchase entitlement. We do not receive your Apple account password or payment-card details through the app.

Apple’s processing of purchase, account, and payment information is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Advertising and tracking

Phone Storage Reclaimer does not use advertising or cross-app tracking. We do not sell your photo or video files or use them for advertising.

Your choices

You can limit or revoke the app’s Photos access through iPhone Settings. Revoking access prevents further access to your library; it does not itself erase information already stored in the app.

Deleting the app removes its local app data from that installation. Media already saved in Photos is managed separately through Photos. Device backups and Apple purchase records are also managed separately under your Apple account and device settings.

Our website

This website is primarily informational and does not currently require visitors to create an account or provide personal information.

Information we may receive

If you contact us by email, we may receive your name, email address, and any information you include in your message. We use that information only to respond to your inquiry and for ordinary business recordkeeping.

You do not need to send your photos or videos to request help with Phone Storage Reclaimer. Please avoid including private media or unnecessary personal information in support messages.

Website hosting and technical data

Our hosting and domain providers may process limited technical information, such as IP addresses, request logs, browser information, and security data, as necessary to operate and protect the website.

Cookies and advertising

This website does not currently use advertising cookies or behavioral advertising. If that changes, this policy will be updated.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our products or data practices. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.
